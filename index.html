Electrical Safety
Before You Open That Outlet

Customers often tell us they took a look at the problem themselves before calling. We understand — nobody wants to pay an electrician if they can avoid it. But working with electricity every day has taught us how quickly it goes wrong, so here is what we want every homeowner to know.

White wiring is not always neutral, and black is not always live

Dozens of people may have worked on the wiring in your home over the years, and not all of them were professionally trained or following proper code. Always test with a non-contact voltage tester before you touch anything.

Low voltage does not mean low risk

Voltage is not the part of electricity that harms you — current is. Low voltage wiring can draw currents well outside the safe range, which is why our electricians are trained in how to handle each situation.

Know when to stop

We care about the Farmington Hills community, and the last thing we want is someone getting injured in a way that could have been prevented. If a job starts to feel beyond you, put the tools down and give us a call.
